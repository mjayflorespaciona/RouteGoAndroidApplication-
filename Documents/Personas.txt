DELITRACK
USER ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

1. Customer
          - Places a delivery order.
          - Provides pickup and delivery addresses.
          - Checks delivery status.
          - Tracks the parcel.
          - Receives delivery updates.
          - Confirms receipt of the parcel.

2. Rider
          - Views assigned deliveries.
          - Views the Nasugbu–Lian delivery route.
          - Shares GPS location during active deliveries.
          - Updates delivery status.
          - Reports delivery problems.
          - Marks parcels as delivered or unsuccessful.

3. Admin
          - Manages customer and rider accounts          .
          - Receives and manages delivery orders.
          - Assigns riders and vehicles.
          - Manages vehicle information and availability.
          - Views GIS maps and the Nasugbu–Lian route.
          - Monitors rider GPS locations.
          - Monitors delivery progress and assignments.
          - Manages pickup and delivery locations.
          - Views delivery reports and rider performance.
