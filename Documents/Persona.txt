DELITRACK
USER ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

1. Customer
	- Create an account and log in.
	- Submit delivery requests.
	- Enter sender and receiver information.
	- Provide pickup and destination addresses.
	- Enter package description, weight, dimensions, and quantity.
	- Receive a unique Tracking ID and delivery receipt.
	- Track package status.
	- View the latest reported rider location when available.
	- View previous and completed deliveries.
	- Confirm package receipt, if implemented.

2. Rider
	- Log in to the Rider Dashboard.
	- View assigned deliveries.
	- Check customer and receiver information.
	- View package weight, dimensions, and handling instructions.
	- Check assigned vehicle information.
	- Follow the designated Nasugbu–Lian route.
	- Share GPS location during active delivery, when enabled.
	- Update delivery status.
	- Report delivery problems.
	- Mark deliveries as Delivered or Failed Delivery.
	- View personal delivery history.

3. Admin
	- Log in through an authorized Admin account.
	- View the Admin Dashboard.
	- Manage customer accounts.
	- Add, edit, and manage rider accounts.
	- Add, edit, and manage vehicle records.
	- Review and manage delivery requests.
	- Assign available riders and suitable vehicles.
	- Check vehicle weight and cargo capacity.
	- Monitor delivery progress.
	- View the GIS map of the Nasugbu–Lian route.
	- Monitor actual or last-reported rider GPS locations.
	- Generate delivery and operational reports.
